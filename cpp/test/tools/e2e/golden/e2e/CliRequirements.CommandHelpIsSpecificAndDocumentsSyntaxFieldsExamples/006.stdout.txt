Usage: tsfile-cli sketch [-o|--output <file>] [--force] <file.tsfile>
Writes the physical layout text from the bound printSketch behavior.
Result fields: printSketch text; --format is not supported.
Default: stdout; --force requires --output and replaces only a regular file.
Examples:
  tsfile-cli sketch data.tsfile
  tsfile-cli sketch -o layout.txt data.tsfile
